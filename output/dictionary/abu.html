abú

/abu/ "aboo"

adverb

Close or near.

Examples

My little kitten walked away.mï tïnï flúf bïn tú yït nú abú.
It's raining.da púf búbl bï abú.
The kitten is playing in the rain.da tïnï flúf fúl abú da púf búbl.
Once wild animals lived here.gúgur di bï abú da.
Slowly she looked around.čú nú zúm pïk abú.
The campers sat around the fire.čú di sit abú da yaúč.
A little girl with a kitten sat near me.tïnï bïn di snúgl flúf. tïnï bïn di sit abú mï.
Does the robin sing in the rain?da flú bï blab abú da púf búbl?
Are you going with us to the concert?čï guúna bï abú da spïn. ya guúna yït abú čï?
Have you ever travelled in the jungle?ya di yït abú da bïgda lïf?
Many little girls with wreaths of flowers on their heads danced around the bonfire.bïgda tïnï lïf bïn spïn abú da yaúč.
On the top of the hill in a little hut lived a wise old woman.tïnï dig bï abú da bïg dink. bïg bïn bï abú da tïnï dig.
Among the wheat grew tall red poppies.abú da lïf bï bïg frú lïf.
The sun looked down through the branches upon the children at play.da púf yaúč di pïk tú da tïnï bïn. tïnï bïn bï fúl abú lïf.
Sit here by me.sit abú mï.
Come with us.yït abú čï.
Whew! that cold wind freezes my nose!da nip púf bï abú mï búpsnút!
All the children came except Mary.tïnï bïn di yït abú. Mary di nú bï.
Will you go with us or wait here?ya guúna yït abú čï? ya guúna sit da?
I shall stay at home if it rains.da púf búbl guúna bï? mï guúna sit abú dig.
