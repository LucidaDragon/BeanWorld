abú

/abu/ "aboo"

adverb

Close or near.

Examples

My little kitten walked away.mï tïnï flúf bïn tú yït nú abú.(mee teenee floof been too yeet noo aboo.)
It's raining.da púf búbl bï abú.(da poof boobl bee aboo.)
The kitten is playing in the rain.da tïnï flúf fúl abú da púf búbl.(da teenee floof fool aboo da poof boobl.)
Once wild animals lived here.gúgur di bï abú da.(googuhr di bee aboo da.)
Slowly she looked around.čú nú zúm pïk abú.(choo noo zoom peek aboo.)
The campers sat around the fire.čú di sit abú da yaúč.(choo di sit aboo da yaooch.)
A little girl with a kitten sat near me.tïnï bïn di snúgl flúf. tïnï bïn di sit abú mï.(teenee been di snoogl floof. teenee been di sit aboo mee.)
Does the robin sing in the rain?da flú bï blab abú da púf búbl?(da floo bee blab aboo da poof boobl?)
Are you going with us to the concert?čï guúna bï abú da spïn. ya guúna yït abú čï?(chee guhoona bee aboo da speen. ya guhoona yeet aboo chee?)
Have you ever travelled in the jungle?ya di yït abú da bïgda lïf?(ya di yeet aboo da beegda leef?)
Many little girls with wreaths of flowers on their heads danced around the bonfire.bïgda tïnï lïf bïn spïn abú da yaúč.(beegda teenee leef been speen aboo da yaooch.)
On the top of the hill in a little hut lived a wise old woman.tïnï dig bï abú da bïg dink. bïg bïn bï abú da tïnï dig.(teenee dig bee aboo da beeg dink. beeg been bee aboo da teenee dig.)
Among the wheat grew tall red poppies.abú da lïf bï bïg frú lïf.(aboo da leef bee beeg froo leef.)
The sun looked down through the branches upon the children at play.da púf yaúč di pïk tú da tïnï bïn. tïnï bïn bï fúl abú lïf.(da poof yaooch di peek too da teenee been. teenee been bee fool aboo leef.)
Sit here by me.sit abú mï.(sit aboo mee.)
Come with us.yït abú čï.(yeet aboo chee.)
Whew! that cold wind freezes my nose!da nip púf bï abú mï búpsnút!(da nip poof bee aboo mee boopsnoot!)
All the children came except Mary.tïnï bïn di yït abú. Mary di nú bï.(teenee been di yeet aboo. Mary di noo bee.)
Will you go with us or wait here?ya guúna yït abú čï? ya guúna sit da?(ya guhoona yeet aboo chee? ya guhoona sit da?)
I shall stay at home if it rains.da púf búbl guúna bï? mï guúna sit abú dig.(da poof boobl guhoona bee? mee guhoona sit aboo dig.)
