guúna

/gəuna/ "guhoona"

adverb

An action that will occur in the future.

Examples

The sun will shine.da púf yaúč guúna bï fúf.
The sun will shine tomorrow.da púf yaúč guúna bïgtïm bï fúf.
Soon the rain will stop.da púf búbl guúna fïn tïnïtïm.
I hope the rain stops soon.da púf búbl guúna fïn tïnïtïm? mï bï ï.
I will be happy to go.mï ï guúna yït.
He will arrive soon.čú guúna fïn yït tïnïtïm.
Can you come tomorrow?ya guúna bïgtïm tú?
Are you going with us to the concert?čï guúna bï abú da spïn. ya guúna yït abú čï?
You and I will go together.čï guúna yït.
Be quick or you will be too late.zúm! ya guúna forgor ya tïm.
Will you go with us or wait here?ya guúna yït abú čï? ya guúna sit da?
We will make this place our home.da guúna bï čï dig.
I shall stay at home if it rains.da púf búbl guúna bï? mï guúna sit abú dig.
Come early next Monday if you can.ya guúna yït tú Monday da. ya guúna bï bïg tïnïtïm?
