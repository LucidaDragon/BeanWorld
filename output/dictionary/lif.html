lïf

/lif/ "leef"

noun

A plant or a piece of plant.

Examples

Have you ever travelled in the jungle?ya di yït abú da bïgda lïf?(ya di yeet aboo da beegda leef?)
Many little girls with wreaths of flowers on their heads danced around the bonfire.bïgda tïnï lïf bïn spïn abú da yaúč.(beegda teenee leef been speen aboo da yaooch.)
Among the wheat grew tall red poppies.abú da lïf bï bïg frú lïf.(aboo da leef bee beeg froo leef.)
The sun looked down through the branches upon the children at play.da púf yaúč di pïk tú da tïnï bïn. tïnï bïn bï fúl abú lïf.(da poof yaooch di peek too da teenee been. teenee been bee fool aboo leef.)
