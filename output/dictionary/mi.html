mï

/mi/ "mee"

noun

The speaker or narrator of the phrase.

Examples

My little kitten walked away.mï tïnï flúf bïn tú yït nú abú.(mee teenee floof been too yeet noo aboo.)
I hope the rain stops soon.da púf búbl guúna fïn tïnïtïm? mï bï ï.(da poof boobl guhoona feen teeneeteem? mee bee ee.)
I will be happy to go.mï ï guúna yït.(mee ee guhoona yeet.)
My cat is black.mï flúf bïn bï tïnïpïk.(mee floof been bee teeneepeek.)
I usually sleep soundly.mï tïnïbïgtïm ï mïrmïr.(mee teeneebeegteem ee meermeer.)
I have been waiting for you.mï di sit. mï gib ya mï tïm.(mee di sit. mee gib ya mee teem.)
A little girl with a kitten sat near me.tïnï bïn di snúgl flúf. tïnï bïn di sit abú mï.(teenee been di snoogl floof. teenee been di sit aboo mee.)
The west wind blew across my face like a friendly caress.da púf di snúgl mï snút.(da poof di snoogl mee snoot.)
I am very happy.mï bï bïg ï.(mee bee beeg ee.)
Are you waiting for me?ya gib mï ya tïm?(ya gib mee ya teem?)
Sit here by me.sit abú mï.(sit aboo mee.)
I am tired, but very happy.mï bï mïrmïr. mï bïg ï.(mee bee meermeer. mee beeg ee.)
Aha! I have caught you!mï di yoink ya!(mee di yohink ya!)
Whew! that cold wind freezes my nose!da nip púf bï abú mï búpsnút!(da nip poof bee aboo mee boopsnoot!)
Come when you are called.mï blab tú ya. ya yït tú mï.(mee blab too ya. ya yeet too mee.)
I shall stay at home if it rains.da púf búbl guúna bï? mï guúna sit abú dig.(da poof boobl guhoona bee? mee guhoona sit aboo dig.)
When he saw me, he stopped.da bïn di pïk mï. da bïn di fïn.(da been di peek mee. da been di feen.)
Do not laugh at me because I seem so absent minded.mï nú tink. ya nú kïk.(mee noo tink. ya noo keek.)
