spïn

/spin/ "speen"

verb

To shake or dance.

Examples

Are you going with us to the concert?čï guúna bï abú da spïn. ya guúna yït abú čï? (chee guhoona bee aboo da speen. ya guhoona yeet aboo chee?)
Tom laughed at the monkey's tricks.Tom kïk tú da flúf gúbur spïn. (Tom keek too da floof goobuhr speen.)
Many little girls with wreaths of flowers on their heads danced around the bonfire.bïgda tïnï lïf bïn spïn abú da yaúč. (beegda teenee leef been speen aboo da yaooch.)
