sit

/sɪt/ "sit"

verb

To remain in place.

noun

An object that holds or supports a thing.

Examples

The kitten jumped onto the table.da flúf bïn tú yït púf. da flúf tú yït sit.(da floof been too yeet poof. da floof too yeet sit.)
I have been waiting for you.mï di sit. mï gib ya mï tïm.(mee di sit. mee gib ya mee teem.)
The campers sat around the fire.čú di sit abú da yaúč.(choo di sit aboo da yaooch.)
A little girl with a kitten sat near me.tïnï bïn di snúgl flúf. tïnï bïn di sit abú mï.(teenee been di snoogl floof. teenee been di sit aboo mee.)
Sit here by me.sit abú mï.(sit aboo mee.)
Will you go with us or wait here?ya guúna yït abú čï? ya guúna sit da?(ya guhoona yeet aboo chee? ya guhoona sit da?)
I shall stay at home if it rains.da púf búbl guúna bï? mï guúna sit abú dig.(da poof boobl guhoona bee? mee guhoona sit aboo dig.)
