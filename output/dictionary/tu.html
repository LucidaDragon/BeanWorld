tú

/tu/ "too"

verb

To come.

adverb

An action occurring toward.

Examples

The sun is rising now.da púf yaúč yït tú púf.
The kitten jumped up.da flúf bïn tú yït púf.
The kitten jumped onto the table.da flúf bïn tú yït púf. da flúf tú yït sit.
My little kitten walked away.mï tïnï flúf bïn tú yït nú abú.
The rain came down.da púf búbl di yït tú dink.
You have come too soon.ya di tú bïg tïnïtïm.
The children ran after Jack.tïnï bïn di yït tú Jack.
We arrived at the river.čï di yït tú da búbl.
Can you come tomorrow?ya guúna bïgtïm tú?
Tom laughed at the monkey's tricks.Tom kïk tú da flúf gúbur spïn.
The sun looked down through the branches upon the children at play.da púf yaúč di pïk tú da tïnï bïn. tïnï bïn bï fúl abú lïf.
Jack seized a handful of pebbles and threw them into the lake.Jack di yoink dink. Jack di yït dink tú búbl.
Come when you are called.mï blab tú ya. ya yït tú mï.
Come early next Monday if you can.ya guúna yït tú Monday da. ya guúna bï bïg tïnïtïm?
