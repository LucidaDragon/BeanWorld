zúm

/zum/ "zoom"

adverb

Quickly or rapidly.

adjective

Fast, quick, or high velocity.

Examples

Slowly she looked around.čú nú zúm pïk abú.(choo noo zoom peek aboo.)
We should eat more slowly.čï nú zúm bïg bred?(chee noo zoom beeg brehd?)
Hurry, children.zúm, tïnï bïn.(zoom, teenee been.)
Be quick or you will be too late.zúm! ya guúna forgor ya tïm.(zoom! ya guhoona fohrgohr ya teem.)
