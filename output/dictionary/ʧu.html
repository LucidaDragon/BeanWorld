čú

/ʧu/ "choo"

noun

The individual involved or previously mentioned.

Examples

Slowly she looked around.čú nú zúm pïk abú. (choo noo zoom peek aboo.)
He will arrive soon.čú guúna fïn yït tïnïtïm. (choo guhoona feen yeet teeneeteem.)
The campers sat around the fire.čú di sit abú da yaúč. (choo di sit aboo da yaooch.)
Their voices sound very happy.čú blab bï bïg ï. (choo blab bee beeg ee.)
