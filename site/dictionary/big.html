bïg

/big/ "beeg"

adjective

A larger, older, or greater thing.

Examples

The sun is shining again.da púf yaúč bïg di bï fúf. da púf yaúč di bï nú fúf. da púf yaúč bï fúf. (da poof yaooch beeg di bee foof. da poof yaooch di bee noo foof. da poof yaooch bee foof.)
All the people shouted.bïgda bïn di bïg blab. (beegda been di beeg blab.)
Many of the people shouted twice.bïgda bïn di bïg blab. bïgda bïn bïg blab. (beegda been di beeg blab. beegda been beeg blab.)
Happy people often shout.ï bïn tïnïbïgtïm bïg blab. (ee been teeneebeegteem beeg blab.)
We should eat more slowly.čï nú zúm bïg num? (chee noo zoom beeg nuhm?)
You have come too soon.ya di tú bïg tïnïtïm. (ya di too beeg teeneeteem.)
On the top of the hill in a little hut lived a wise old woman.tïnï dig bï abú da bïg stïf. bïg bïn bï abú da tïnï dig. (teenee dig bee aboo da beeg steef. beeg been bee aboo da teenee dig.)
Among the wheat grew tall red poppies.abú da lïf bï bïg frú lïf. (aboo da leef bee beeg froo leef.)
I am very happy.mï bï bïg ï. (mee bee beeg ee.)
Their voices sound very happy.čú blab bï bïg ï. (choo blab bee beeg ee.)
He is small, but strong.bïn bï tïnï. bïn bï bïg. (been bee teenee. been bee beeg.)
I am tired, but very happy.mï bï mïrmïr. mï bïg ï. (mee bee meermeer. mee beeg ee.)
Come early next Monday if you can.ya guúna yït tú Monday da. ya guúna bï bïg tïnïtïm? (ya guhoona yeet too Monday da. ya guhoona bee beeg teeneeteem?)
