guúna

/gəuna/ "guhoona"

adverb

An action that will occur in the future.

Examples

The sun will shine.da púf yaúč guúna bï fúf. (da poof yaooch guhoona bee foof.)
The sun will shine tomorrow.da púf yaúč guúna bïgtïm bï fúf. (da poof yaooch guhoona beegteem bee foof.)
Soon the rain will stop.da púf búbl guúna fïn tïnïtïm. (da poof boobl guhoona feen teeneeteem.)
I hope the rain stops soon.da púf búbl guúna fïn tïnïtïm? mï bï ï. (da poof boobl guhoona feen teeneeteem? mee bee ee.)
I will be happy to go.mï ï guúna yït. (mee ee guhoona yeet.)
He will arrive soon.čú guúna fïn yït tïnïtïm. (choo guhoona feen yeet teeneeteem.)
Can you come tomorrow?ya guúna bïgtïm tú? (ya guhoona beegteem too?)
Are you going with us to the concert?čï guúna bï abú da spïn. ya guúna yït abú čï? (chee guhoona bee aboo da speen. ya guhoona yeet aboo chee?)
You and I will go together.čï guúna yït. (chee guhoona yeet.)
Be quick or you will be too late.zúm! ya guúna forgor ya tïm. (zoom! ya guhoona fohrgohr ya teem.)
Will you go with us or wait here?ya guúna yït abú čï? ya guúna sit da? (ya guhoona yeet aboo chee? ya guhoona sit da?)
We will make this place our home.da guúna bï čï dig. (da guhoona bee chee dig.)
I shall stay at home if it rains.da púf búbl guúna bï? mï guúna sit abú dig. (da poof boobl guhoona bee? mee guhoona sit aboo dig.)
Come early next Monday if you can.ya guúna yït tú Monday da. ya guúna bï bïg tïnïtïm? (ya guhoona yeet too Monday da. ya guhoona bee beeg teeneeteem?)
