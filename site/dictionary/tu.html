tú

/tu/ "too"

verb

To come.

adverb

An action occurring toward.

Examples

The sun is rising now.da púf yaúč yït tú púf. (da poof yaooch yeet too poof.)
The kitten jumped up.da flúf bïn tú yït púf. (da floof been too yeet poof.)
The kitten jumped onto the table.da flúf bïn tú yït púf. da flúf tú yït sit. (da floof been too yeet poof. da floof too yeet sit.)
My little kitten walked away.mï tïnï flúf bïn tú yït nú abú. (mee teenee floof been too yeet noo aboo.)
The rain came down.da púf búbl di yït tú stïf. (da poof boobl di yeet too steef.)
You have come too soon.ya di tú bïg tïnïtïm. (ya di too beeg teeneeteem.)
The children ran after Jack.tïnï bïn di yït tú Jack. (teenee been di yeet too Jack.)
We arrived at the river.čï di yït tú da búbl. (chee di yeet too da boobl.)
Can you come tomorrow?ya guúna bïgtïm tú? (ya guhoona beegteem too?)
Tom laughed at the monkey's tricks.Tom kïk tú da flúf gúbur spïn. (Tom keek too da floof goobuhr speen.)
The sun looked down through the branches upon the children at play.da púf yaúč di pïk tú da tïnï bïn. tïnï bïn bï fúl abú lïf. (da poof yaooch di peek too da teenee been. teenee been bee fool aboo leef.)
Jack seized a handful of pebbles and threw them into the lake.Jack di yoink stïf. Jack di yït stïf tú búbl. (Jack di yohink steef. Jack di yeet steef too boobl.)
Come when you are called.mï blab tú ya. ya yït tú mï. (mee blab too ya. ya yeet too mee.)
Come early next Monday if you can.ya guúna yït tú Monday da. ya guúna bï bïg tïnïtïm? (ya guhoona yeet too Monday da. ya guhoona bee beeg teeneeteem?)
